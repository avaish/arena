WHAT THE REPLAY SAYS

Songs become timestamps.

The through-line is attachment, not genre. A song tends to stick after it gets tied to a movie, a trip, a person, a neighborhood, or one very specific year.

Bollywood, J-rock, ’80s pop, movie music, and heavier detours can all sit together comfortably. The playlist reads more like autobiography than genre sorting.
