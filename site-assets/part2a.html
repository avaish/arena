THE DATING PART

Plans are easy. Company is the point.

Museums close. Games end. Eventually there is just the walk home.

"WE SHOULD GO"

That sentence tends to become a date and time.

Exhibit, game, ferry, movie, class, museum tour: once an idea sounds good, logistics usually follow.

MENTION IT ONCE

It may reappear on the calendar.

A passing February idea can return in April with two available Thursdays. Details have a long shelf life.

TUESDAY NIGHT

Ordinary plans count.

Dinner at home, a walk, grocery shopping, one episode turning into three, or a very serious discussion about a minor neighborhood development.

YOUR TURN

Reciprocity is attractive.

Bring plans too. A very good text: “Found something weird. Thursday?” Strong opinions and specific enthusiasms welcome.

THE ACTUAL POINT

Marriage, kids, and a lot of conversation.

Looking for someone curious, communicative, affectionate, dependable, with her own obsessions and enough confidence to suggest the better subway route.
